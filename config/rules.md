## Criticism is welcome

This community collects feedback about EQ: Legends.

Criticism, disagreement, and negative feedback about the game are welcome.
Explain what you think and why. **Negative sentiment alone is not grounds for moderation.**

## Respect other people

Discuss ideas and the game, not other people's worth. Do not post:

- Harassment, threats, or hate speech.
- Someone else's private information.
- Spam or unrelated advertising.

## Keep the discussion useful

Keep contributions relevant and include examples where helpful.
Community managers may lock or archive posts that break these rules.
